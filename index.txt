<section class="story">

<div class="story-item">
<div class="story-title">The Client</div>

<p>
The client is a U.S.-based medical device design and engineering agency supporting companies through regulated product development, from early concept and prototyping through manufacturing readiness. Its work is especially relevant for organizations that need specialized FDA-aware design support without building every capability internally.
</p>

<p>
The agency had strong technical credibility and a service offering suited to both emerging medical device startups and established OEM teams with niche project demands. However, commercial growth was still largely dependent on existing relationships and referrals.
</p>
</div>

<div class="story-item">
<div class="story-title">Target Audience</div>

<p>
The campaign focused on medical device startup CEOs and founders, product managers, engineering staff, and project or program leaders responsible for moving regulated devices through development. Outreach also targeted established medical device OEMs seeking specialized project support for niche or capacity-constrained initiatives.
</p>

<p>
These buyers and stakeholders were selected because they directly influence partner evaluation, project staffing, product development timelines, and outsourced engineering decisions within medical device organizations.
</p>
</div>

<div class="story-item">
<div class="story-title">The Problem</div>

<p>
Before working with Lucid Strategies, the client did not have a dedicated lead generation system. New opportunities came primarily through referrals, which limited visibility into new markets and made pipeline growth difficult to scale.
</p>

<p>
The company also lacked organized marketing and sales processes, including a more structured operating rhythm for outreach, follow-up, and lead handling. As a result, the business had strong expertise but no repeatable outbound engine to consistently create qualified conversations.
</p>
</div>

<div class="story-item">
<div class="story-title">The Mechanism</div>

<p><strong>Hyper-Targeted Prospect Database</strong><br>
Lucid Strategies built a focused prospect database around medical device startups, OEM decision-makers, and technical leaders most likely to need compliance-aware design and engineering support.</p>

<p><strong>Multi-Channel Outreach</strong><br>
The campaign used coordinated outreach across cold calls, email, and LinkedIn to create repeated visibility with relevant decision-makers while keeping messaging aligned to the client’s niche expertise.</p>

<p><strong>Structured Outreach Cadence</strong><br>
Each prospect entered a structured outreach cadence of up to 555 touches across multiple communication channels, creating a disciplined system for consistent follow-up and stronger response coverage over time.</p>

<p><strong>Qualification and Pipeline Management</strong><br>
Lucid Strategies supported inbox handling, lead qualification, and ongoing campaign management so the client could stay focused on technical delivery while engaging prospects who matched the right project profile.</p>
</div>

<div class="story-item">
<div class="story-title">The Result</div>

<div class="result-callout">
Across the tracked campaign period, Lucid Strategies generated <strong>859+ leads</strong> and <strong>23+ qualified appointments</strong>, while also producing <strong>22+ warm leads</strong>, <strong>6 hot leads</strong>, and <strong>2 email information requests</strong>.
</div>

<p>
The campaign gave the client a clearer path beyond referral-based growth and demonstrated that there was measurable demand among medical device startups and OEM teams for specialized design and engineering support. More importantly, it validated that the market responded when the agency’s value proposition was presented to the right audiences through a structured outbound system.
</p>

<p>
Rather than relying on sporadic introductions, the client gained a more scalable mechanism for creating relevant sales conversations, testing message-market fit, and building a healthier business development pipeline inside the medical device sector.
</p>
</div>

<div class="story-item">
<div class="story-title">Definitions</div>

<p>
<strong>Email Information Request</strong><br>
Occurs when a prospect expresses interest during outreach and requests additional information via email.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Sample Request</strong><br>
Occurs when a qualified prospect requests a physical product sample to evaluate before making a purchasing decision.
</p>
</div>
